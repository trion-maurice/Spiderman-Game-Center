Reservation Policy

  • Booking recommended on weekends and holidays
  • Party packages require at least 3 days advance booking
  • 50% deposit required for party bookings

  • Full payment required for standard passes
  • Confirmation email sent within 24 hours

What to Bring

  • Swimming costume and towel for pool activities
  • Comfortable clothes and socks for play areas
  • Information about special dietary requirements
  • Sunscreen for outdoor activities
  • Water bottle (refill stations available)
  • Valid ID for check-in (for adults)

Safety Guidelines

  • Children must be supervised by adults at all times
  • Pool area requires adult supervision for children under 8
  • First aid station available on-site
  • All staff trained in child safety protocols
